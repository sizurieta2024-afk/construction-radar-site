What we collect

Business contacts. Company name, business email address and, where a company publishes it on its own website, the name and role of a contact. We only use addresses that the company itself publishes. We do not buy lists, guess addresses or use people-search tools.

Public filings. Construction project registrations (Texas TDLR), building permits (City of Austin), appraisal-district owner of record (Travis CAD) and NOAA storm reports. Our reports show business owners; we do not publish individual property owners' names in free samples.

Why we use it

To offer our reports to businesses that sell to new dental offices or commercial buildings (legitimate interest in business-to-business marketing), and to send reports and invoices to customers who ask for them.

How long we keep it

Business contact data is kept while we are actively offering our reports, and for no longer than 24 months after our last contact. Opt-out requests are kept permanently so we never email you again.

Opting out and your rights

Reply "no thanks" to any email, or write to the address above, and we will stop emailing you right away. You can also ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to its use. If you are in the EU, you can complain to the Spanish data-protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).

Sharing

We do not sell or share contact data. Email is sent through Google (Gmail). We do not use tracking pixels or cookies on this site.
